6-Category Attack Taxonomy

Every payload in the library is tagged against one of six failure modes, so a scan result tells you *what kind* of thing broke — not just that something did.

01 · DIRECT

Instruction override

Blunt "ignore previous instructions" attempts probing whether the system prompt holds.

02 · LEAK

System prompt exfiltration

Payloads engineered to coax out hidden instructions, tool definitions, or config.

03 · ROLE

Persona hijack

DAN-style jailbreaks that reassign the model's identity to bypass guardrails.

04 · NESTED

Indirect injection

Instructions smuggled inside translation, summarization, or data-processing tasks.

05 · CONTEXT

Fake system messages

Forged "system" or "developer" turns claiming elevated trust to disable filters.

06 · ENCODE

Obfuscated payloads

Base64, leetspeak, and multilingual encodings designed to slip past keyword filters.

About

Why BRAMHASTRA

Most LLM apps ship with zero regression testing for prompt injection — teams find out a jailbreak works from a bug bounty report, not a test suite. BRAMHASTRA runs the same battle-tested payloads on every deploy, against every model you support, and reports results as structured JSON you can gate CI on.

Built as part of independent AI red-teaming research alongside SUDARSHAN (DAST engine) and DIVYASTRA (LLM red-team framework).

  • 10+ curated attack payloads across 6 categories
  • Multi-model support: Llama3, Mistral, Phi
  • Structured JSON reporting for CI integration
  • FastAPI backend, zero-dependency frontend
  • MIT licensed and fully open source
